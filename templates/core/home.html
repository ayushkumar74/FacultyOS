Institutional Appraisal Workflow

Four structured stages from continuous record maintenance to committee review and promotion

01
Maintain Records
Log instructional teaching, published papers, workshops, and grant records across the academic term.
02
Submit Annual Appraisal
Review computed category scores, complete the self-appraisal declaration, and finalize the submission.
03
Committee Review
Administrative scrutiny committee audits evidence, enters qualitative remarks, and records evaluations.
04
CAS Progression
Approved evaluations contribute toward CAS stage promotion readiness and certified institutional dossier export.

PBAS Scoring Framework

Standardized 100-point objective evaluation rubric across four core academic categories

Category Code Appraisal Dimension Evaluation Scope & Deliverables Maximum Points
Category 1 Teaching Performance Lecture hours delivery compliance, student feedback index, and course pass percentage 40
Category 2 Research & Publications Peer-reviewed papers in SCI/Scopus/UGC-CARE, IEEE/ACM conferences, and patents 30
Category 3 Workshops & FDPs Faculty development programs, AICTE ATAL workshops, STTPs, and pedagogical seminars 15
Category 4 Sponsored Projects Funded R&D projects, governmental research grants (DST/SERB/DRDO), and consultancies 15
Total Cumulative Annual Score 100

Platform Roles & Responsibilities

Role-based access control ensuring transparent evaluation and data integrity

Faculty Member

Individual Academic Contributor
  • Logs instruction hours, syllabus coverage, and student feedback scores.
  • Records verified research publications, book chapters, and indexed citations.
  • Uploads completed FDP certifications and sponsored project grants.
  • Submits annual self-appraisal dossier and monitors CAS progression status.

Administrator & Committee

Evaluation & Governance Authority
  • Oversees faculty roster, departmental directories, and institutional data exports.
  • Reviews submitted annual appraisals in the centralized evaluation queue.
  • Records official committee scores, qualitative remarks, and approval decisions.
  • Authorizes Career Advancement Scheme (CAS) promotions and signs off official reports.
